## What USSIDs are

**USSID = United States SIGINT System Directive.** Issued by the Director, NSA / Chief, CSS, USSIDs govern how the US SIGINT System (USSS) operates — collection, processing, analysis, retention, dissemination, and protection.

The USSID numbering scheme groups directives by subject area:
- **1xx** — broad policy and authority (e.g., USSID 18 US-person rules; USSID 109).
- **2xx** — collection-discipline / target-area directives.
- **6xxx** — specific mission programs.

---

## Where USSID 201 sits in the series

The "2xx" range covers **collection management and target development** — the discipline that turns intelligence requirements into specific collection tasking against specific targets. Companion directives in this range cover how to register targets, how to track collection performance, how to share collection responsibilities across the SIGINT enterprise, and how to deconflict among collectors.

USSID 201's specific subject matter is **classified** and must be looked up on the SIGINT classified systems where the document lives.

## Common testable concepts (collection-discipline theme)

When the Bib pairs USSID 201 with target-development and collection-management adjacent material, the general testable themes are:

- **Collection management** — translating prioritized intelligence requirements into specific collection tasking; matching collectors to targets.
- **Target development** — turning a named entity into a tasked target with selectors, history, and assessed value (parallels JP 3-60 joint targeting concepts at the SIGINT level).
- **Collection deconfliction** — multiple collectors / agencies / Five Eyes partners working similar targets must not duplicate effort or step on each other's operations.
- **Performance tracking** — yield against tasking; whether the collection is producing intelligence of value to the customer.
- **Tasking formats** — formal request and tasking documents flow through the USSS; informal handoffs occur but are exceptions, not the rule.
