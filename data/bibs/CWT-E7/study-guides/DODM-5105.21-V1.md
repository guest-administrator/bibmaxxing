# DODM 5105.21 Volume 1 — SCI Administrative Security Manual

**Bib study scope:** Enclosure 4.

**Source on disk:** `DODM-5105.21 Vol 1 SCI Administrative Security Manual.pdf`.

## Why this matters {#why}

If you hold a **Top Secret/SCI** clearance and access SCI spaces — and almost every CWT operational billet does — this is the manual that governs how you handle, safeguard, transmit, and destroy SCI. Violations can end your career and constitute a federal crime.

## The SCI system {#sci-system}

### What SCI is

**Sensitive Compartmented Information** is classified intelligence information requiring special controls beyond the baseline classification level. SCI is always **Top Secret** (the SCI designation doesn't exist at lower classifications — they use other control markings like NOFORN).

SCI is partitioned into **compartments** (e.g., SI for SIGINT, TK for imagery, HCS for HUMINT) and **subcompartments** (GAMMA within SI, etc.).

### Access requirements

- **TS clearance** — Tier 5 background investigation.
- **SCI eligibility** — adjudicated per **ICD 704** (Intelligence Community Directive 704).
- **Nondisclosure agreement** — SCI-specific NDA (**Form 4414**).
- **Read-on / indoctrination** — for each compartment the person needs access to, formally briefed by the SSO.
- **Need to know** — access only to compartments/information required for the mission.
- **Continuous evaluation** — enrolled in the Continuous Vetting program.

### SSO — Special Security Officer

Every organization with SCI access has an **SSO** — the individual responsible for managing SCI:

- Granting/revoking SCI access.
- Maintaining SCI personnel security records.
- Running the SCIF (if one exists).
- Reporting compliance + incidents.
- Coordinating read-ons / read-offs.

## The SCIF {#scif}

A **Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility** is the physical space where SCI is stored, processed, or discussed.

### Physical security (ICD 705)

**ICD 705** sets SCIF construction standards — walls, doors, locks, sound attenuation, TEMPEST, visitor controls. Key requirements:

- **Accredited** by a cognizant security authority.
- **Access-controlled** — badge + PIN or equivalent; visitors logged.
- **Sound attenuation** — conversations inside can't be heard outside.
- **No unauthorized electronics** — personal phones, fitness trackers, smartwatches typically banned.
- **Alarmed** during non-operational hours.
- **Inspected** periodically.

### Classifications inside

SCIFs can store/process SCI up to TS//SI//TK or other compartments, per the accreditation.

## Enclosure 4 — expected content {#enclosure-4}

Enclosure 4 in DoD Manuals typically covers **procedures** specific to the manual's subject. For DODM 5105.21 Vol 1 "SCI Administrative Security," Enclosure 4 likely covers either:

- **SCI Information Handling Procedures** — marking, storage, transmission, destruction specific to SCI (beyond the baseline classification rules in SECNAV M-5510.36).
- **SCI Reproduction and Control** — counts, serialization, approved devices.
- **SCI Derivative Classification** — applying compartment markings correctly.

The principles map closely to SECNAV M-5510.36 but with **additional controls** for SCI:

- SCI-only storage containers (GSA Class 5 minimum, typically).
- Courier requirements — DCS or specifically approved personnel with SCI courier cards.
- No SCI on non-SCI IT systems ever (obvious but reinforced).
- **MFD/NFI/ORCON** handling per originator specifications.

## SCI transmission {#transmission}

Strict: only by approved means.

- **Defense Courier Service (DCS)** — the baseline for SCI hand-carry across long distances.
- **Authorized courier** — with SCI courier card, briefed on requirements, transporting in approved container.
- **Electronic transmission** — on JWICS (Joint Worldwide Intelligence Communications System) or equivalent TS//SCI network; never on SIPRNET or NIPRNET.
- **Never by commercial mail, email, or phone.**

## SCI destruction {#destruction}

NSA-approved methods:

- **Cross-cut shredder** meeting specific particle-size standards (typically 1 mm × 5 mm for SCI hardcopy).
- **Pulverization** for robust paper destruction.
- **Burning** at an approved facility.
- **Digital destruction** — degaussing, physical destruction of drives. **Wiping is generally NOT sufficient for SCI** — physical destruction is standard.

## Reporting requirements {#reporting}

SCI-accessed personnel must report to the SSO:

- Foreign travel (personal) — **required pre-travel reporting** + post-travel debrief.
- Foreign contacts — close and continuing.
- Suspicious approaches (potential recruitment) — SAEDA report.
- Classified spills involving SCI.
- Loss or compromise of SCI material.
- Changes in personal circumstances (marriage, divorce, financial difficulty, arrests).

## Incidents and their reporting {#incidents}

### Classified spill of SCI

A **spill of SCI to a non-SCI system** (email to SIPR, posting on NIPR, etc.) is one of the most serious compliance events. Immediate actions:

1. **Contain** — identify all systems/users touched.
2. **Notify** — SSO + security manager + command leadership immediately.
3. **Document** — timeline, content summary (not the content itself), affected systems.
4. **Clean** — coordinated technical response, often requiring re-imaging, sanitization.
5. **Report** — through established channels to DoD / IC authorities (ODNI, NSA CSS as applicable).

### Compromise

If SCI is disclosed to unauthorized recipients:

- **CI investigation** (Counterintelligence) often opens.
- **Damage assessment** conducted.
- Individual responsible faces administrative + potential criminal action.

### Unauthorized SCI possession

Even accidental possession (taking SCI material home) is an incident. Self-reporting immediately minimizes consequences.

## Courier procedures {#courier}

SCI couriers must:

- Hold appropriate clearance + access.
- Be briefed + hold a courier card.
- Transport in approved lockable briefcase or container.
- Maintain positive control at all times (not left in vehicles, hotel rooms unattended).
- Know destruction procedures for emergencies (approved fires, sensitive-document-destruction kits).
- Document the movement in the SCI accountability system.

## TEMPEST {#tempest}

**TEMPEST** is the U.S. government codename for standards designed to prevent electromagnetic emanation exploitation — i.e., preventing the leak of classified info via electromagnetic signals that could be captured outside the SCIF.

SCIF construction and equipment approval includes TEMPEST considerations:

- **Zoning** — proximity of SCIF to external threat.
- **Shielding** — building materials and equipment treatments.
- **Approved equipment lists** — equipment certified for use at specific levels.

## Exam-testable concepts

- What's SCI? **Sensitive Compartmented Information — intelligence requiring special controls beyond baseline classification.**
- What clearance level does SCI require? **TS (Top Secret).**
- Who manages SCI in a command? **SSO — Special Security Officer.**
- What directive sets SCIF construction standards? **ICD 705.**
- What ICD governs SCI eligibility determinations? **ICD 704.**
- What's the SCI NDA form? **Form 4414.**
- What network is TS//SCI? **JWICS.**
- Can SCI ride on SIPRNET? **No — SIPR is Secret, not SCI-cleared.**
- How is SCI transmitted between distant locations? **DCS (Defense Courier Service) or approved courier — never commercial mail.**
- What's TEMPEST? **U.S. standards for preventing electromagnetic emanation compromise of classified info.**
- First action when you realize you've accidentally spilled SCI to a non-SCI system? **Contain, notify SSO/security manager/leadership immediately, document.**
