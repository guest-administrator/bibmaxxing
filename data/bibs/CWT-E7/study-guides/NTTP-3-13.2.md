# NTTP 3-13.2 — Navy Information Operations Warfare Commander's Manual

> **⚠ Reference summary, not a full study guide.** NTTP 3-13.2 is CUI/FOUO. Its parent NWP 3-13 (public, 2014) is on disk as adjacent material.

**Bib study scope:** Appendix G.
**Classification / Availability:** Controlled distribution — NIPR/MyNavy Portal via command access.

## What this document is {#what-it-is}

NTTP 3-13.2 is the **Information Operations Warfare Commander (IWC) Manual** — the NTTP that describes how the IWC (a warfare commander in a Navy task force / strike group) organizes, plans, executes, and assesses information operations. Within an Expeditionary Strike Group, Carrier Strike Group, or Maritime Headquarters, the IWC is the single-point commander responsible for all information-related capabilities and information warfare effects in the AOR.

## The IWC role {#iwc-role}

The IWC is a **composite warfare commander** alongside the AIR Warfare Commander (AWC), Sea Combat Commander (SCC), and Strike Warfare Commander (STWC) in the composite warfare construct. The IWC:

- Integrates the five core IRCs (EW, MISO, MILDEC, OPSEC, cyberspace ops) for the force commander.
- Synchronizes with the intelligence and operations staffs.
- Provides the IO staff estimate into the overall operational plan.
- Owns the force's information warfare posture for the operation.

Billet profile: an O-6 (Captain) or senior O-5 from the Information Warfare Community (typically a 1810 Cryptologic Warfare Officer, 1820 Information Professional, or 1830 Intelligence designator). The IWC commands an IW staff across the CT, IT, IS, and METOC disciplines and reports to the Composite Warfare Commander (normally the Carrier Strike Group Commander for a CSG).

## The Composite Warfare Commander (CWC) construct {#cwc-construct}

CWC is the Navy's **command-by-negation** framework for a task force at sea. The CWC retains command but delegates warfare areas to subordinate Warfare Commanders, who operate with standing authority unless the CWC negates a specific decision. The warfare commanders in a typical carrier strike group:

| Warfare Commander | Warfare area | Typical billet |
|---|---|---|
| **CWC** | Composite — overall authority | Strike Group Commander |
| **AWC (Air Warfare Commander)** | Air defense | CAG / ship CO |
| **SCC (Sea Combat Commander)** | Surface + subsurface warfare | Destroyer squadron commander |
| **STWC (Strike Warfare Commander)** | Strike (Tomahawk, air-delivered) | CAG / staff |
| **IWC (Information Warfare Commander)** | Information operations + IW | O-6 IW community |
| **MIW (Mine Warfare Commander)** | Mine warfare (when applicable) | MIW community |
| **USW (Undersea Warfare Commander)** | Subsurface (alternative to SCC) | DESRON CO or submarine rep |

The IWC's elevation to full warfare-commander parity was a deliberate 2010s shift recognizing that the information environment is a warfighting domain co-equal with air, sea, and strike.

## IWC staff organization {#iwc-staff}

Though size varies by platform, a typical IWC staff includes:

- **Deputy IWC** — O-5, runs daily ops.
- **IW Plans** — deliberate and crisis-action planning, maintains the IO annex.
- **IW Current Ops** — executes tonight's plan; drives the IWC watch floor.
- **Cryptology / SIGINT** — CTs working collection, processing, reporting.
- **Cyber Operations cell** — liaison to Navy CMF teams; executes tactical cyber effects when authorized.
- **EW officer** — coordinates EA/ES with ships, embarked aircraft, off-board shooters.
- **MISO / MILDEC liaison** — integrates influence effects (often via a joint MISO cell if available).
- **OPSEC officer** — protects friendly critical information.
- **METOC** — meteorology / oceanography; informs EM propagation, SATCOM planning, ASW conditions.
- **Intelligence integration** — pairs with the CWC's N2 / intel staff.

## IWC battle rhythm and boards {#battle-rhythm}

A mature IWC battle rhythm typically runs these standing events (exact names vary by strike group):

- **Daily IWC stand-up** — staff sync; priorities for the ops day.
- **IWC Targeting Board** — prioritizes IRC effects against the adversary target set; feeds the CWC targeting board.
- **IO Working Group (IOWG)** — cross-functional coordination with fires, intel, PA, comms.
- **Effects Assessment Review** — MOPs and MOEs against ongoing IRC objectives.
- **IWC CDR VTC** — IWC reports up to CWC / higher.
- **Synchronization matrix update** — ties IRC actions to the campaign timeline.

## IWC interaction with higher echelons {#higher-echelons}

Above the strike group IWC sits:

- **Numbered fleet IWC / IO chief** (e.g., U.S. SEVENTH Fleet, U.S. FIFTH Fleet, U.S. SIXTH Fleet) — theater-level IO guidance.
- **FLTCYBERCOM/TENTH Fleet** — Navy cyber and IW operational command; owns the Navy CMF pipeline.
- **JFHQ-Cyber / USCYBERCOM** — joint cyber tasking for Navy forces.
- **Joint Force Maritime Component Commander (JFMCC) or CCMD IO cell** — theater integration.

The IWC passes approved IRC effects requests (especially cyber effects outside Navy-owned cyberspace) up this chain because DCO-RA and OCO authorities rest with USCYBERCOM / SECDEF / POTUS, not with the strike group.

## What's publicly known about the IWC concept

The **Composite Warfare Commander (CWC) construct** is unclassified Navy doctrine publicly described in NWP 3-56 (public-sanitized versions exist) and professional writing in USNI Proceedings. Key facts:

- The Commander, Task Force (CTF) or Composite Warfare Commander (CWC) delegates warfare areas to subordinate warfare commanders: AWC, SCC, STWC, IWC.
- The IWC was established as a formal warfare commander role in the early 2010s as the Navy elevated Information Warfare to parity with Air, Sea, Strike.
- IWC is the Navy's operational-level embodiment of the Information Warfare Community (IWC) enlisted/officer specialties: CTs, IPs, METOCs, IDC officers, and others.

## Relationship to CWC-IWC acronym collision {#acronym-collision}

Be careful: **IWC** is used for both:

- **Information Warfare Commander** (the NTTP 3-13.2 billet).
- **Information Warfare Community** (the cluster of ratings / designators).

Both are routinely abbreviated IWC. Exam questions usually give enough context to disambiguate, but when in doubt: **Information Warfare Commander = the billet on a strike group staff; Information Warfare Community = the HR umbrella for CTs, IPs, ISs, METOCs, 1810/1820/1830 officers**.

## Exam-testable concepts

- What warfare commander integrates IRCs for a Navy task force? **Information Operations Warfare Commander (IWC)**.
- Which composite warfare commander is the IWC peer to? **AWC, SCC, STWC** (and sometimes MIW, USW, etc.).
- Which community provides IWC staff personnel? **Information Warfare Community** (CTs, IPs, METOCs, IDWO officers).
- What are the five core IRCs the IWC integrates? **EW, MISO, MILDEC, OPSEC, Cyberspace Operations**.
- In the CWC construct, what authority model describes warfare-commander delegation? **Command by negation** — warfare commanders act with standing authority unless the CWC negates a specific decision.
- Who is the IWC's immediate superior on a carrier strike group? **Composite Warfare Commander (CWC)** — normally the Strike Group Commander.
- Where does the IWC get operational cyber authority from? **USCYBERCOM through FLTCYBERCOM** — the IWC does not organically hold DCO-RA or OCO authority.
- Typical paygrade of an IWC? **O-6 (Captain) or senior O-5** from the IW community.
