# JP 2-01 — Joint and National Intelligence Support to Military Operations

**Bib study scope:** Chapter II; Executive Summary.

**Source on disk:** `JP-2-01 Joint and National Intelligence Support.pdf`.

## Why this matters {#why}

JP 2-01 is the joint publication that explains **how national intelligence supports joint military operations** — the pipeline from national agencies (NSA, CIA, DIA, NGA, NRO) through combatant command J2s down to the warfighter. As a CWT Chief inside the IW community, you're part of this pipeline.

## The Intelligence Community (IC) {#ic}

Seventeen IC members (plus ODNI), but the core IC members a Chief must know:

| Agency | Primary discipline | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| **CIA** | HUMINT | Human source intelligence; covert operations |
| **DIA** | All-source, military | All-source fusion for DoD |
| **NSA/CSS** | SIGINT | Signals intelligence (communications + electronic) |
| **NGA** | GEOINT | Geospatial intelligence, imagery |
| **NRO** | Technical collection | Satellites + sensor systems (operator of national collection platforms) |
| **FBI** | Domestic intel + counterintel | Counterintelligence, domestic terrorism, cybercrime |
| **Service intel** | Service-specific | ONI (Navy), INSCOM (Army), AFISR/AF IC (Air Force), MCIA (Marine Corps), CGI (Coast Guard) |
| **ODNI** | Coordination | Director of National Intelligence, head of IC |
| **State INR** | Diplomatic intel | State Dept's intelligence arm |
| **DEA, DHS, DOE, Treasury** | Niche intel | Each agency's specific intel office |

## The intelligence cycle (Chapter II core content) {#cycle}

The classic six-step cycle:

1. **Planning and Direction** — commander's requirements become PIRs (Priority Intelligence Requirements).
2. **Collection** — sensors + sources gather raw information.
3. **Processing and Exploitation** — raw collection is converted into usable form (decrypting, translating, image-processing).
4. **Analysis and Production** — analysts interpret and produce finished intelligence.
5. **Dissemination and Integration** — finished intel delivered to consumers.
6. **Evaluation and Feedback** — consumers assess utility; requirements update.

Note: modern treatments sometimes use an **8-step cycle** adding **Requirements** as a standalone step before planning. Both are correct; the 6-step is more commonly tested.

## The intelligence disciplines (INTs) {#ints}

| INT | Name | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **SIGINT** | Signals Intelligence | Electromagnetic signals (COMINT, ELINT, FISINT) |
| **HUMINT** | Human Intelligence | People |
| **GEOINT** | Geospatial Intelligence | Imagery + geospatial data |
| **MASINT** | Measurement and Signature Intelligence | Scientifically derived signatures |
| **OSINT** | Open-Source Intelligence | Publicly available information |
| **TECHINT** | Technical Intelligence | Foreign military equipment/tech |
| **CI** | Counterintelligence | Identifying + countering foreign intel threats |

**SIGINT sub-types (critical for CTs):**
- **COMINT** — Communications Intelligence (intercepted conversations).
- **ELINT** — Electronic Intelligence (non-communication emissions — radars, beacons).
- **FISINT** — Foreign Instrumentation Signals Intelligence (telemetry, tracking signals).

## All-source analysis {#all-source}

**All-source** means fusing multiple INTs to produce finished intelligence. No single INT gives complete picture:

- SIGINT might say "adversary unit is active at location X."
- GEOINT might confirm units are present visually.
- HUMINT might reveal the unit's intentions.
- OSINT might confirm the unit's historical pattern.
- MASINT might characterize the unit's equipment signatures.

Fusion through an all-source process produces confidence-rated conclusions.

## Joint intelligence organization {#org}

### J2 at every echelon

Every joint force has an **Intelligence Directorate (J2)**:

- **CCMD J2** — supports CCDR with theater-level intel.
- **JTF J2** — supports JTF commander.
- **Component J2s** — service-component intel.

### Joint Intelligence Operations Center (JIOC)

Each CCMD has a **JIOC** — 24/7 all-source intelligence center supporting the CCDR. The JIOC is the operational interface to the national IC.

### Joint Intelligence Support Element (JISE) / Joint Intelligence Center (JIC)

Subordinate JTFs stand up a JISE to provide dedicated intelligence to the JTF commander.

## IPB — Intelligence Preparation of the Battlespace {#ipb}

**IPB** (now often called **Intelligence Preparation of the Operational Environment, IPOE**) is the analytical methodology used by J2s:

1. **Define the operational environment** — geography, systems, actors.
2. **Describe environmental effects** on operations — weather, terrain, civil, infrastructure.
3. **Evaluate the threat** — adversary capabilities, vulnerabilities, composition.
4. **Determine threat courses of action** — most likely + most dangerous adversary COAs.

IPB feeds the JPP's Mission Analysis step directly.

## Commander's Critical Information Requirements (CCIRs) {#ccirs}

Commander establishes CCIRs to focus intelligence:

- **Priority Intelligence Requirements (PIRs)** — what the commander needs to know about the **enemy and environment** to decide and act.
- **Friendly Force Information Requirements (FFIRs)** — what the commander needs to know about **friendly forces**.

PIRs drive the collection plan — each PIR translates into specific intelligence requirements, then into collection tasking against specific sensors/sources.

## Dissemination mechanisms {#dissemination}

- **SIGINT reporting** — KLIEGLIGHT, TACTICAL reports, formal reports (through appropriate channels).
- **GEOINT products** — imagery, maps, geospatial databases.
- **All-source reports** — INTSUMs (Intelligence Summaries), INTREPs (Intelligence Reports), estimates.
- **Secure channels** — JWICS (TS/SCI), SIPRNet (Secret), NIPRNet (unclass).
- **CRITIC** — highest-priority flash reporting to national leadership (see CRITIC Handbook summary).

## The Time-Sensitive Cycle {#ts-cycle}

For perishable intel (imminent threat, fleeting target):

1. **Detect** — sensor or source reports.
2. **Assess** — rapidly characterize significance.
3. **Report** — FLASH-precedence or CRITIC as appropriate.
4. **Disseminate** — to the commander who can act.
5. **Act** — decision or engagement.

Cyberspace operations often feed this cycle — identifying fleeting adversary cyber activity that triggers both intel reporting and possible DCO response.

## Collection management {#collection-mgmt}

Between requirements and collection sits **collection management** — the discipline of translating intel needs into executable sensor/source tasking:

1. **Requirements Management (RM)** — validate, prioritize, assign tracking numbers to incoming PIRs/SIRs.
2. **Collection Operations Management (COM)** — match requirements to collection capabilities; task sensors/sources.
3. **Collection Strategies** — how to layer multiple INTs for redundancy and confidence.
4. **Collection Management and Dissemination (CM&D)** — the executing office at J2.

Navy afloat: the **ISIC** (Immediate Superior in Command) and strike group J2 shop coordinate collection requests against national-level collection; tactical SIGINT aboard ships serves organic collection needs.

## PIR → SIR → EEI waterfall {#pir-sir-eei}

Commander's requirements decompose into executable tasks:

| Level | Full | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **PIR** | Priority Intelligence Requirement | "Will adversary attack CSG before D+14?" |
| **SIR** | Specific Information Requirement | "Are adversary surface units deploying from home port?" |
| **EEI** | Essential Element of Information | "Photograph destroyer hulls at XYZ naval base in last 72 hrs" |
| **ISR task** | Sensor-specific | "NGA imagery tasking on XYZ base for past 72 hrs" |

Each level is more specific. By the time a requirement becomes an ISR task, it's directly executable.

## Classification markings for intel {#classification}

Intel products carry specific markings:

- **Classification level** — TOP SECRET / SECRET / CONFIDENTIAL / UNCLASSIFIED.
- **SCI control system** — SI (SIGINT), TK (TALENT KEYHOLE / satellite), HCS (HUMINT Control System), etc.
- **Dissemination controls** — NOFORN (no foreign), ORCON (originator controls), REL TO (releasable to specific allies).
- **Caveats** — e.g., //FVEY (Five Eyes), //USA ONLY.

A CWT working SIGINT products deals with TS//SI//NOFORN, TS//SI//REL TO USA, FVEY, and similar combos routinely. Mismarking is a compliance violation.

## Intelligence oversight {#oversight}

Intel activity is heavily overseen:

- **EO 12333** — foundational IC activities authority (1981, updated).
- **DoD 5240.01** — DoD intelligence activities, procedures governing DoD intel components.
- **DoD 5240.01-R Procedures 1–10 (now 15)** — procedures for DoD intel components touching U.S. persons.
- **USSID 18** — SIGINT U.S. person protections (NSA/CSS).
- **FISA** — foreign intelligence surveillance on U.S. persons.
- **Congressional oversight** — SSCI, HPSCI, Armed Services committees.

CWT E-7 is personally responsible for conduct inside these frameworks — ignorance is not a defense.

## Exam-testable concepts

- Six steps of the intel cycle? **Planning & Direction → Collection → Processing & Exploitation → Analysis & Production → Dissemination & Integration → Evaluation & Feedback.**
- Name three SIGINT sub-disciplines. **COMINT, ELINT, FISINT.**
- What does MASINT stand for? **Measurement and Signature Intelligence.**
- What's OSINT? **Open-Source Intelligence — publicly available.**
- Which IC agency owns SIGINT? **NSA/CSS.**
- Which IC agency owns GEOINT? **NGA.**
- Which IC agency owns satellite collection platforms? **NRO.**
- What's a CCMD's 24/7 intel center called? **JIOC — Joint Intelligence Operations Center.**
- PIR vs FFIR? **PIR = what we need to know about enemy/environment; FFIR = what we need to know about friendly forces.**
- What analytical process feeds JPP Mission Analysis? **IPB / IPOE.**
- What decomposes a PIR into specific sensor tasking? **SIR → EEI → ISR task.**
- What foundational EO governs IC activities? **EO 12333.**
- What DoD manual governs intel component conduct toward U.S. persons? **DoD 5240.01 / 5240.01-R.**
- What marking means "releasable only to named allies"? **REL TO [nation codes].**
- What marking prohibits release to any foreign party? **NOFORN.**
