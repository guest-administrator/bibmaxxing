## What USSIDs are

**USSID = United States SIGINT System Directive.** Issued by the Director, NSA / Chief, CSS, USSIDs are the operational and procedural rulebook for the US SIGINT System (USSS). They cover how SIGINT is collected, processed, analyzed, retained, disseminated, and protected.

USSIDs are categorized by series — the leading digits group related subject matter (collection management, target development, US persons protections, regional operations, technical SIGINT, etc.).

## Common testable concepts (general SIGINT compliance, not USSID 109-specific)

The Bib often pairs USSIDs with the US-person protections theme. General compliance concepts the CWT should know cold:

- **Authority chain:** Title 50 → EO 12333 → DoD 5240.1-R → USSIDs → unit standing operating procedures.
- **US-person rules:** intentional collection of US-person communications requires specific authority; incidental collection requires minimization.
- **Foreign intelligence purpose:** all SIGINT collection must serve a foreign intelligence or counterintelligence purpose.
- **Need-to-know:** dissemination of US-person identities is restricted to recipients with specific need-to-know.
- **Reporting:** suspected violations are reported through the IG / OGC channel without delay.
